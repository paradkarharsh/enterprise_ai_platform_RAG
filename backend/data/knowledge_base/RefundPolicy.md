# TechMart Electronics Refund Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your product, you may return it according to the guidelines below.

## Return Period
- **Standard Electronics**: Returns are accepted within **30 days** of delivery.
- **Open-Box / Clearance Items**: Returns are accepted within **14 days** of delivery.
- **Software & Digital Licenses**: All digital products and software licenses are non-refundable once the license key has been revealed or sent via email.

## Return Conditions
1. **Original Condition**: Items must be returned in their original packaging, with all accessories, cables, user guides, and promotional items included.
2. **Restocking Fee**: A **15% restocking fee** is applied to any opened item that is returned without its original packaging, or exhibits signs of mild wear.
3. **Return Shipping**: TechMart provides free return shipping labels for defective merchandise. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs ($9.99 flat rate deducted from refund value).

## Refund Process
- **Processing Time**: Once our warehouse receives and inspects your return, it takes between **5 to 7 business days** to approve the refund.
- **Reimbursement Method**: Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method. Depending on your financial institution, it may take an additional 3 to 5 business days for the funds to reflect in your account.
