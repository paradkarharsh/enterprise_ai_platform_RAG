# TechMart Electronics Shipping Policy

TechMart ships to customers across North America, Europe, and selected international regions.

## Order Processing
- Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** are processed and handed off to our carrier partners (FedEx, UPS, USPS) on the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, or during weekends and public holidays, will be processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Methods & Costs (Domestic USA)
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: FREE for orders over $49 | $5.99 flat rate for orders under $49. Delivery time: **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Expedited Shipping (2-Day)**: $14.99 flat rate. Delivery time: **2 business days**.
- **Overnight Shipping**: Price calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Delivery time: **Next business day** (must be ordered before 12:00 PM EST for same-day dispatch).

## International Shipping
- Shipping fees and delivery windows are calculated dynamically at checkout based on destination country and customs duty selections.
- Customs duties and import fees are calculated and collected upfront at checkout to ensure hassle-free delivery.
