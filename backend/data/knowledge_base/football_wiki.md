# Football & FIFA World Cup 2022 Wiki

## FIFA World Cup 2022 Overview
The FIFA World Cup 2022 was the 22nd edition of the tournament, hosted by Qatar from November 20 to December 18, 2022. It was the first World Cup held in the Arab world and the second held entirely in Asia.

## The Final Match
Argentina won the FIFA World Cup 2022 after defeating France in the final match. The game was played at the Lusail Stadium and ended in a 3-3 draw after extra time. Argentina won the subsequent penalty shootout 4-2 to claim their third World Cup title.

## Key Players and Awards
- **Lionel Messi**: Lionel Messi captained Argentina to victory and was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player, scoring seven goals.
- **Kylian Mbappé**: France's Kylian Mbappé won the Golden Boot as the top goalscorer with eight goals, including a hat-trick in the final.
- **Emiliano Martínez**: Argentina's goalkeeper won the Golden Glove award as the tournament's best goalkeeper.
- **Enzo Fernández**: Argentina's young midfielder was awarded the Young Player Award.
