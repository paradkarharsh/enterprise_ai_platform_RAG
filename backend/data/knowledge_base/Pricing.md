# TechMart Electronics Pricing & Plans

TechMart Electronics offers a variety of products, subscription plans, and premium membership tiers to suit personal and business needs.

## Premium Tech Membership (TechPlus)
Get access to member-only prices, priority support, and extended return periods.

### Plans & Tiers
- **TechPlus Starter**: **$9.99 / month**
  - Benefits: Free standard shipping on all orders, 5% cash back, and 30-day extended returns.
- **TechPlus Premium**: **$19.99 / month**
  - Benefits: Free 2-day shipping, 10% cash back, 45-day extended returns, and priority live chat routing.
- **TechPlus Business**: **$49.99 / month**
  - Benefits: Free next-day shipping on bulk items, dedicated account manager, business tax exemptions, and customized corporate pricing.

## Volume Discounts
Corporate accounts buying in bulk are eligible for the following discounts:
- **10 - 24 Units**: 5% discount on checkout price.
- **25 - 99 Units**: 10% discount on checkout price.
- **100+ Units**: 15% discount and dedicated freight delivery.
