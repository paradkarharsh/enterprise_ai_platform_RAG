# TechMart Electronics Warranty Information

All products sold by TechMart Electronics are covered by our Limited Warranty program in addition to standard manufacturer warranties.

## Coverage Periods
- **TechMart Certified Refurbished**: **90-Day Limited Warranty**.
- **Brand New Smart Devices & Electronics**: **1-Year Limited Warranty** from the date of purchase.
- **High-End Audio & Premium Computing Gear**: **2-Year Limited Warranty**.

## What is Covered?
Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. If a hardware defect occurs and a valid claim is received within the warranty period, TechMart will:
1. Repair the hardware defect at no charge using new or refurbished replacement parts.
2. Exchange the product with a product that is new or refurbished.
3. Refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not feasible.

## What is NOT Covered?
- Cosmetic damage, including scratches, dents, and broken plastic on ports.
- Damage caused by accident, abuse, misuse, liquid contact, fire, or earthquake.
- Damage caused by operating the product outside the user manual guidelines.
- Damage caused by service performed by anyone who is not an authorized TechMart technician.
- Software issues, firmware downgrades, or data loss.

## How to Submit a Claim
1. Contact our Support Hotline or open a ticket.
2. Provide your order number, serial number, and a detailed description of the defect.
3. If approved, we will send you a pre-paid shipping label to send the defective unit back to our repair center.
