## Orders & Tracking

### How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, we will send you a confirmation email containing a tracking link. You can also view details under the "Order History" section of your TechMart account dashboard.

### Can I modify or cancel my order?
Orders are processed rapidly by our automated fulfillment center. You can cancel or modify your order within 30 minutes of placement by calling our phone hotline directly. After 30 minutes, the order is locked and must be handled via our return process.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and interest-free installment options via Affirm or Klarna.
