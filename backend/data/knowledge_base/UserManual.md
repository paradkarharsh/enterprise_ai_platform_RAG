# TechMart Electronics General User Manual

This manual provides troubleshooting steps and usage guidelines for smart devices purchased from TechMart.

## Troubleshooting Smart Connectivity Issues

### Hub Connection Offline
If the TechLink Smart Hub v2 is offline (LED indicator is solid red or blinking red):
1. **Check Power Connection**: Ensure the power adapter is firmly plugged into both the wall outlet and the hub.
2. **Reboot Router**: Unplug your internet router, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in.
3. **Power Cycle Hub**: Unplug the power cord from the hub, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in.
4. **Firmware Update**: Check the app settings to see if a firmware update is pending. Do not power off the hub during updates.

### Camera Connection Failures
If the TechSecure Smart Camera drops connection repeatedly:
1. **Range Verification**: Ensure the camera is within 50 feet of the TechLink Smart Hub. Thick concrete walls and metal siding can reduce this distance.
2. **Battery check**: Inspect battery levels in the app. A battery below 10% can cause connection dropping.
3. **Sync Reset**: Press the sync button on the camera for 10 seconds to restore factory defaults, then re-pair via the app.

## Factory Reset Procedures

### Factory Reset: TechLink Smart Hub v2
- Locate the pinhole reset button on the back of the hub.
- Insert a paperclip and hold the button down for **15 seconds** until the LED indicator flashes red three times.
- Release the button. The hub will reboot and the LED will blink amber, indicating it is ready to be set up as a new device.
- *Note: Factory resetting deletes all paired accessories (like smart plugs and cameras) from the hub's memory.*
