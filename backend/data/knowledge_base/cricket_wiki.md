# Cricket & Greats Wiki

## Cricket Legend: Sachin Tendulkar
Sachin Tendulkar is widely referred to as the "God of Cricket" or the GOAT (Greatest of All Time) of cricket. He holds the records for scoring the most runs in both Test matches and One Day Internationals (ODIs) and is the only player in cricket history to score 100 international centuries.

## The Greatest Batsman: Sir Donald Bradman
Sir Donald Bradman of Australia is universally acknowledged as the greatest batsman in cricket history. His career Test batting average of 99.94 is widely regarded as the greatest achievement in any major sport.

## Modern Great: Virat Kohli
Virat Kohli is considered one of the greatest modern-day batsmen, famous for his chase statistics, batting consistency, and aggressive captaincy style across all three formats of international cricket.

## Captain Cool: MS Dhoni
Mahendra Singh Dhoni (MS Dhoni) is regarded as one of cricket's most successful captains. Under his leadership, India won the 2007 ICC World Twenty20, the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy, making him the only captain to win all three ICC limited-overs trophies.
