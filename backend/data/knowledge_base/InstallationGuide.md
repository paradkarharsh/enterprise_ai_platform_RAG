# TechMart Electronics Smart Devices Installation Guide

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for installing and setting up your TechLink Smart Hub v2 and TechSecure Smart Camera.

## 1. Setting up the TechLink Smart Hub v2
### Steps:
1. **Unboxing & Placement**: Place the hub in a central location in your home, at least 3 feet away from your primary Wi-Fi router to avoid signal interference.
2. **Power Up**: Connect the power adapter to the hub and plug it into a standard electrical outlet. The LED light indicator will blink amber, indicating it is ready to pair.
3. **App Configuration**: Download the "TechMart Smart Home" app from the App Store or Google Play Store.
4. **Network Connection**: Open the app, select "Add Device", and scan the QR code located on the bottom of your hub. Follow the app instructions to connect the hub to your 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi network.
5. **Final Pairing**: The LED light will turn solid green once it is connected to our servers.

## 2. Setting up the TechSecure Smart Camera
### Steps:
1. **Charging**: Charge the camera fully before mounting. The charging LED indicator turns green when complete.
2. **Mounting**: Choose a mount location with a clear field of view. Secure the mounting bracket to the wall or ceiling using the provided screws.
3. **Attach Camera**: Screw the camera body onto the mounting bracket and adjust the angle.
4. **Pair with Hub**: Open the TechMart app, ensure your TechLink Hub is online, select the Hub, and choose "Add Accessory". Press the sync button on the bottom of the camera for 3 seconds until the LED light flashes blue.
5. **Live Feed Test**: Check the app to verify the live streaming feed and calibrate motion detection zones.
